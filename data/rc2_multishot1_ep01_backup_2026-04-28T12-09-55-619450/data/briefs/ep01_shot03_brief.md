# Episode 01, Shot 03 Brief

## Shot ID
ep01_shot03

## Scene Goal
Reveal Alya's first moment of magical awakening as she touches the crystal.

## Visual Description
As Alya's fingers make contact with the crystal, brilliant blue energy swirls around her hands and up her arms. Her eyes widen with realization as the forest around her transforms - flowers bloom instantly, and gentle motes of light dance in the air. The camera pulls back to show the entire clearing radiating with newfound magic.

## Voiceover Text
The moment her skin touched the crystal's surface, she felt it - a power that had been dormant within her, waiting for this precise instant to awaken. The forest responded to her presence, as if the very land had been waiting for its true guardian to finally emerge.
